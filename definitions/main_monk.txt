`main_monk` is the subject of the biography, named in full in its first sentence; after that, the text usually calls him by the last character of his name alone ({last} for {name}). Read each sentence in the context of the biography. A sentence that tells what others said of him or how they responded to him is about him. A sentence that tells what someone else did or was (a disciple, a teacher, a layman, a ruler, another monk), mentioning him only in passing, is about that other person. A sentence in a passage that introduces another person and goes on to tell that person's learning, character or career is part of that person's own account, even when it says he studied under `main_monk` or followed his work.
